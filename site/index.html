How it works

Five stages. Four of them never touch the network.

Transcribe

Word timestamps from Apple's on-device speech engine, or Whisper on older Macs.

on your Mac

Pick the moments

Every candidate clip starts and ends on a complete sentence. A language model scores hook, payoff and whether it stands alone.

transcript text only
Apple on-device or your provider

Follow the speaker

Face tracking with a 233 KB model keeps whoever is talking in the vertical frame. Two people on screen get a split.

on your Mac

Caption

Word-synced captions in four styles, drawn with bundled fonts so they look the same on every machine.

on your Mac

Render and export

Hardware H.264, loudness normalised to −14 LUFS, fast-start MP4, plus a poster, subtitles and a metadata file per clip.

on your Mac

Clip picker

Choose where the judgment runs. You pay the provider, never us.

Privacy

Your video never leaves your Mac.

The only thing that can go over the network is transcript text, and only to the clip picker you chose. There is no choco server. No account, no telemetry, nothing to leak.

video file→ speech to text→ transcript text → your picker→ face tracking→ captions + render→ clips on disk

Open source

Read it, build it, change it.

The engine, the command-line tool and the app are MIT licensed in one repository. The video stack is LGPL FFmpeg, dav1d, whisper.cpp and the YuNet face detector; captions use the Inter and Montserrat typefaces.
