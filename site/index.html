Features

Everything a clip needs, done locally.

Each stage runs on your Mac. Only the transcript text goes to the clip picker you choose.

Picks whole thoughts

Clips start and end on complete sentences, never mid-word. A language model scores each one for hook, payoff and whether it stands alone.

Follows the speaker

Face tracking keeps whoever is talking in the vertical frame, with smooth moves. Two people on screen get a split layout.

Word-synced captions

Four styles: clean, bold, karaoke and minimal. Fonts ship with the app, so captions look identical on every Mac.

Broadcast-ready audio

Loudness normalised to −14 LUFS, the level TikTok, Reels and Shorts expect. Hardware H.264 with fast-start MP4.

Ask for a topic

Type "clips about pricing" and only matching moments are scored. No match means no charge.

Files ready to post

Every clip comes with a poster frame, SRT and VTT subtitles, and a title, caption and hashtags you can copy.

How it works

Five stages. Four stay offline.

Transcribe

Apple's built-in speech engine, or Whisper on older Macs.

on device

Pick

Candidate moments scored by the picker you chose.

transcript text only

Reframe

Find faces, track the active speaker, plan the crop.

on device

Caption

Short chunks timed to each word, drawn with bundled fonts.

on device

Export

9:16, 1:1, 4:5 or 16:9, at 5× realtime on Apple Silicon.

on device

Privacy

Your video never leaves your Mac.

There is no choco server, no account and no telemetry. Your provider key lives in the macOS Keychain. The only thing that can leave your machine is transcript text, and only to the picker you picked.
