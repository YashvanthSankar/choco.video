Features

An editor's eye, without the editing.

Every stage runs on your Mac. Only the transcript text goes to the clip picker you choose.

Clips that start and end on a thought

Candidates begin where a sentence begins and stop where it lands. A language model scores each one for hook, payoff and whether it makes sense on its own.

…and that's basically it. The best startup ideas look like bad ideas at first. That's why nobody else is doing them. Anyway, so…

Framing that follows the speaker

Face tracking finds who is talking and moves the vertical frame with them, smoothly. Two people in one shot get a split layout.

Speaker A
Speaker B

Ask for a topic

Type "clips about pricing". Only moments that mention it are scored, and no match costs nothing.

Sound at platform level

Loudness normalised to −14 LUFS, hardware H.264, fast-start MP4.

Ready to post

Each clip ships with a poster, SRT and VTT subtitles, and a title, caption and hashtags.

Captions

Four styles, timed to the word.

Fonts ship inside the app, so a clip looks the same on every Mac. Change the style after the fact without transcribing again.

on device

Caption

on device

Cost

Free app. Pennies for the picker.

Picking clips needs a language model. Use Apple's on-device model at no cost, or bring any OpenAI-compatible key and pay the provider directly.

Privacy

Your video never leaves your Mac.

There is no choco server, no account and no telemetry. The only thing that can leave your machine is transcript text, and only to the picker you picked.
