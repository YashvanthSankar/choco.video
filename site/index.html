    Questions, answered.

    Privacy
    No. Transcription, face tracking, captions and rendering all run on your Mac. The only thing that can leave it is transcript text, sent to the clip picker you choose. Pick Apple's on-device model and nothing leaves at all.
    Macs
    Apple Silicon Macs on macOS 14 or later. On macOS 26 it uses Apple's built-in speech engine with nothing to download; older systems fetch one 550 MB Whisper model the first time.
    Price
    Yes, and MIT licensed. There is no account and no subscription. If you use a hosted picker you pay that provider directly, about two cents per hour of video with the recommended model.
    Quality
    On five public podcasts, two of the recommended model's top five picks matched clips an editor marked, on average. Every pick comes with its reasons, so you can judge it in seconds. The test set is still awaiting human review.
    Output
    A 1080 by 1920 MP4 with captions burned in and sound at −14 LUFS, a poster frame, SRT and VTT subtitles, and a title, caption and hashtags ready to paste. Square, 4:5 and 16:9 too.
