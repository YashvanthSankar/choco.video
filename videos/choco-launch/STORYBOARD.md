## Video direction

- **Palette** (frame.md roles): cream canvas is the ground; cocoa ink is the voice; caramel accent is the ONE voltage per frame (the crop box, the active caption word, the CTA). Footage is the only full-colour element; it is never tinted.
- **Type**: display role for statements (sentence case, tight tracking), mono role for every number, timecode and model name. Numbers are real and come from the run metadata.
- **Motion grammar**: long-tail power3 settles, no bounce. Reveals are paced to the on-screen statement (silent film: each line is the cue); nothing enters before its line. Holds are still.
- **Rhythm**: Frame 3 is the climax (most motion); Frame 4 and Frame 6 are deliberate held breathers; Frame 7 ends still on the CTA.
- **Footage rule**: real choco.video output and its real source only, shown full quality inside soft-cornered frames with a hairline border; never re-captioned or recoloured.
- **Negative list**: no stock imagery, no AI gradients, no fake UI numbers, no logo glyph (the logo is not final; use the text wordmark). No slideshow (front-load then freeze) and no screensaver (everything floating).
- **Keep-out**: content in the top 83%; the bottom band holds only the source credit on Frame 7.
