## Intent

The hero video for the choco.video landing page: a muted, looping 40-second promo that
proves the product with its own real output instead of illustrations. Calm, premium,
warm (chocolate palette), confident. It must read with the sound off.

## Assets

- real clips rendered by choco.video from "Pretty Big Deal with Ashley Graham" (CC BY 3.0,
  Wikimedia Commons) and the Nikola Danaylov interview (CC BY 3.0) — the proof beats.
- the matching 16:9 source footage for one clip — the "before" of the before/after beat.
- site/index.html — brand tokens, copy, numbers (20 MB, ~2¢ per hour, 42x realtime).

## Customizations

- Silent: no narration, no music (website hero autoplays muted). On-screen type carries the story.
- Credit line for the CC BY footage on the last frame.

## Notes

- Do not invent features or numbers; everything shown must exist in the product.
- Built without sub-agents (the user asked to limit agent usage); Codex may build frames.
